> Made-up example: the slides Sam showed on the invented call.

# Slide 1: What makes a team renew?
First look at the renewals analysis. Draft for discussion.

# Slide 2: The data
- Every account that came up for renewal in the last 18 months (just over 2,000)
- Billing records joined to usage logs
- Duplicate sign-ups merged, keeping the account with billing history

# Slide 3: Renewal by plan size
- Small, medium and large plans renew at similar rates
- Plan size is not the main driver

# Slide 4: Usage in the first month
- Teams that log in and create projects more often renew more often
- These measures overlap heavily

# Slide 5: A second person changes everything
- Teams with a second person on the account renew far more often
- Only 17% of one-person teams add a second person on their own

# Slide 6: How many one-person teams are there?
- About one team in three is still a one-person team

# Slide 7: Timing
- Most second people join within the first 15 days of the account
- After that, it becomes much less likely

# Slide 8: Renewal by industry
- Small groups; differences may be noise

# Slide 9: Does it hold up?
- Split by sign-up month: the same pattern in every month

# Slide 10: Three options to try
1. **Invite reminder**: an email a few days in that says "invite a teammate"
2. **Setup call**: a short call with a new account, adding a second person during the call
3. **Tip inside the app**: a small prompt on the projects page suggesting an invite
